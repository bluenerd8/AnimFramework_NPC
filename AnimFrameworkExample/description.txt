Example content mod for AnimFramework, showing how to use it on the vanilla Guide. Replaces his animations with real-time,
uncapped-FPS sprite animation: Idle, Walking, Attacking, Talk, an occasional special idle,
blinking, and an "Alert" variant of his idle and walk poses when enemies are nearby.

The sprite sheet is currently placeholder art. Swap Assets/Guide_Sheet.png (and adjust
Guide_Sheet.json) for real sprites; no code changes needed.

Requires AnimFramework.
